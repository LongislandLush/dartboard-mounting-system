# Assembly Guide — Dartboard Mounting System

**Status:** Prototype planning guide only. **Wall installation is on hold pending safety validation.**

## 1. Prepare and Check Parts
Use the latest SolidWorks exploded drawing and BOM. Verify all purchased dimensions against CAD, especially the ED110 hanging slots, side fixing holes, D-ring screw holes, and Z-bracket clearances. Dry-fit eight Hinoki panels in a 4-column × 2-row arrangement to form a 720 × 600 mm board.

## 2. Fit Birch Connectors
Position three rear and four front 100 × 100 × 9 mm birch plates. Check that each intended fastener enters solid wood rather than a seam, and that front/rear fasteners do not collide. Check wood-grain orientation and potential seasonal movement before rigidly connecting multiple solid-wood panels.

## 3. Prepare Joining Holes
The current CAD uses nominal Ø3.4 mm clearance/countersink holes in birch and initial Ø2 mm × 7 mm pilot holes in Hinoki for Ø3 × 16 mm pointed wood screws. These are **trial dimensions**, not validated fastening specifications. Measure actual screw geometry, drill scrap samples with a depth stop, check splitting and head seating, and verify that no screw breaks through the 10 mm front surface. Do not substitute M3 tapped holes for pointed wood screws.

## 4. Fit ED110 and EVA
Measure the real ED110 before final drilling. Confirm both hanging points and both countersunk fixing holes. Dry-fit four 2 mm EVA front pads, three 2 mm rear pads, and the 10 mm protective surround. Avoid EVA in critical screw-clamping surfaces and do not bond Command strips to EVA. Use adhesive compatible with the foam and wood and follow its curing instructions.

## 5. Fit D-Rings and Printed Brackets
Check the selected D-ring model and its actual base thickness, hole size, and rating. The proposed Ø3 × 8 mm screws in 10 mm Hinoki provide only short engagement and **must not be assumed safe for primary hanging loads**. Fit M3 heat-set inserts into the printed Z-brackets using the insert manufacturer's hole recommendations. Confirm M3 × 4 adjustment screw engagement, travel, anti-loosening measures, and contact protection against the painted wall.

## 6. Wall-Mounting Hold Point
**Do not proceed to wall installation based on the CAD alone.** Approximately 4.2 kg of equipment will experience impacts and outward forces when darts are removed. Four adhesive hooks may not share loads equally; rated picture-hanging values cannot be directly transferred to this configuration. Validate a mechanically secure mounting/fall-prevention solution and the actual wall substrate first.

## 7. Prototype Acceptance Record
After a safe support solution is available, record measured dimensions, fastener condition, seam alignment, ED110 engagement, bracket adjustment range, EVA compression, movement under normal operation, and any cracking or adhesive failure. Update CAD, BOM, and `design-validation.md` with test evidence.
